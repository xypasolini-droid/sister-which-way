# Original paper-theater artwork

Generated for this prototype using the built-in imagegen tool. No film frames or film character art are included in these assets. Original generated files are preserved at the tool's default destination.

## Assets

- `scene-cave.png` — 1672 × 941, RGB. Layered violet cave, empty stage floor.
- `scene-forest.png` — 1672 × 941, RGB. Deep red floral forest, empty stage floor.
- `scene-banquet.png` — 1672 × 941, RGB. Green garden banquet with empty plates, open center.
- `hero.png` — 1024 × 1536, RGBA. Ochre-cloaked child; transparent background.
- `companion.png` — 1024 × 1536, RGBA. Teal-dressed companion; transparent background.

Both character images have real alpha channels. RGB values outside the silhouette may contain generated color; those pixels have alpha 0 and should be rendered as transparent. Keep the alpha channel intact.

## Prompt direction

All assets use a sophisticated original fairy-tale paper theater: visible hand-cut edges, matte gouache, fibrous paper grain, restrained tiny cast shadows, flat geometric shapes. Avoid text, UI, watermarks, shiny 3D, and photorealism.

### Cave

Finished wide 16:9 background illustration for a sophisticated storybook videogame. An original theatrical purple cave constructed from intricately hand-cut layers of textured paper and painted gouache. Layered arching rock walls and curled fern-like foliage frame a deep indigo opening; pale gold pinprick stars. Muted aubergine, dusty violet, midnight blue, ivory highlights. Frontal little paper theater with a broad walkable floor at the lower fifth. Keep center-lower region open for characters and procedural paths. Rich detail concentrated around framing foliage and upper arches. No characters or text.

### Forest

Finished wide 16:9 background illustration of an original red-flower forest made entirely of hand-cut textured paper and matte gouache. Frontal theater stage composition, dark open middle, broad muted burgundy floor along lower fifth. Mysterious but compassionate atmosphere. No characters or text.

### Banquet

Rolling moss-green paper floor at the lower quarter, central foreground empty for characters. Gentle hopeful warm light. No characters or text.
